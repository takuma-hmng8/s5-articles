# 音楽（Suno 用プロンプト）

インスト曲のみ。歌詞なし。

---

## プロンプト（長め）

```
Instrumental only, no vocals. Electronic ambient in the vein of Max Cooper and Nils Frahm: fluid, organic, and emotionally ambiguous. Melancholic pads and soft arpeggios that suggest loss and transformation—something once solid slowly dissolving into something else, like two streams merging into a new pattern. Subtle pulse around 120 BPM, not dance-heavy; the rhythm should feel like a heartbeat or a slow breath. Texture should feel both digital and human: crystalline synths, gentle noise, and evolving layers. Mood: acknowledge sadness and displacement, but leave a thread of possibility—as if after the blend, an unnamed new pattern might still emerge. Minimal, introspective, suitable for a contemplative visual experience.
```

## プロンプト（短め）

```
Instrumental, no vocals. Electronic ambient like Max Cooper: fluid, melancholic but with a hint of hope. 120 BPM, soft pulse, pads and arpeggios. Theme: identity dissolving and blending into something new—loss and quiet possibility. Minimal, introspective.
```

---

## メモ（自分用）

- テーマ: 自分が混ざって消える／変容、喪失と「その先のまだ名のない模様」の可能性（theme.md）
- 曲調: 悲しみだけでも希望だけでもない、曖昧な感情
- 参考: Max Cooper 系の有機的で流動的な電子音楽（theme.md より）
- BPM 120 は実装側のビートと合わせる想定（Scene.tsx）
- 「聴いている音楽も AI」という層が作品の文脈と一致する
